#### 公式
- (2-47)
  \[
  \begin{aligned}
  \hat{r}(t) &= \sum_{k=1}^{K} h_k^*(-t) \otimes r_k(t) \\
  &= \sum_{k=1}^{K} h_k^*(-t) \otimes (h_k(t) \otimes s(t) + w_k(t)) \\

  \end{aligned}
